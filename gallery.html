ENGINEERING SPECIFICATIONS

Core Engine Capabilities

Precision mathematical modeling and real-time physical instrument simulation.

Real-Time MNA Circuit Engine

  • High-precision Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) matrix solver
  • Trapezoidal integration for dynamic capacitors & inductors
  • Instantaneous node voltage & branch loop current computation
  • Variable DC power supply & AC signal generator (Sine/Square/Triangle)

Dual-Channel Digital Oscilloscope

  • Dual trace channels (CH1 Yellow, CH2 Blue)
  • Variable timebase scaling: 10µs/div to 500ms/div
  • Voltage attenuation: 10mV/div to 20V/div
  • Trigger modes: AUTO, NORM, and SINGLE edge capture

Precision Digital Multimeter (DMM)

  • 4 measurement modes: DCV, ACV, DCA, and Resistance (Ω)
  • Interactive movable probes with color-coded leads
  • High-precision 4-digit auto-ranging digital LCD readout
  • Built-in continuity buzzer and diode forward drop test

Rich Electronic Component Library

  • Passives: Resistors (E12/E24 series), Capacitors, Inductors
  • Semiconductors: Diodes, Zener Diodes, LEDs, NPN/PNP Transistors
  • Logic ICs: 7400 NAND, 7402 NOR, 7404 NOT, 7408 AND, 7432 OR
  • Op-Amps: LM741 inverting & non-inverting amplifier configurations

System Compatibility

Requires Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher. Optimized for ARM64 & x86_64 architectures with hardware acceleration.
