Technical Summary

Core Engine Capabilities

Summary of the engineering modules powering the eLab simulator.

Real-Time MNA Circuit Engine

  • High-precision Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) solver
  • Trapezoidal integration for capacitors & inductors
  • Instantaneous node voltages and loop current computation
  • Variable DC power supply & AC signal generator (Sine/Square/Triangle)

Dual-Channel Oscilloscope

  • Dual trace channels (CH1 Yellow, CH2 Blue)
  • Variable timebase: 10µs/div to 500ms/div
  • Voltage attenuation: 10mV/div to 20V/div
  • Trigger modes: AUTO, NORM, and SINGLE edge capture

Precision Digital Multimeter

  • 4 measurement modes: DCV, ACV, DCA, and Resistance (Ω)
  • Interactive movable probes with color-coded leads
  • High-precision 4-digit auto-ranging digital LCD readout
  • Built-in continuity buzzer and diode forward drop test

Components & Safety

  • Standard color-coded resistors, ceramic/electrolytic caps
  • Diodes, LEDs with color luminescence, 555 timers, switches
  • 100% safe environment for students and educators
  • Offline-first operation with zero internet required
